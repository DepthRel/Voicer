Speech to text and back.
On your own computer.

Voicer turns the speech of audio and video files into text, and a text into natural speech. Whisper and free voice models run on your video card or processor: no cloud, no account, nothing leaves the machine.

What Voicer does

Speech to text

Drop an MP3, WAV, MP4, MKV or any other media file and get its text, in about a hundred languages. Export to Markdown.

Text to speech

Paste a script, pick a voice or give a sample of your own, and get a narration up to tens of minutes long.

History and search

Every text and narration is kept with its sound. Find a record by words or by meaning and listen from that moment.

Summaries and questions

A language model on your own server sums up a long meeting and answers questions about your records, with references.

Your video card or processor

Vulkan on Windows and Linux, Metal on Apple silicon. No video card? The light models run well on the processor.

Private by design

Recordings never leave the computer. Models are downloaded once, checked by SHA-256 and kept next to the program.
